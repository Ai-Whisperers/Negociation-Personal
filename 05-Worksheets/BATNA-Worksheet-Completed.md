## What is BATNA?

**BATNA = Best Alternative To a Negotiated Agreement**

Your BATNA is what you'll do if this deal doesn't happen. The stronger your BATNA, the more negotiating power you have.

**Key Principle:** Never accept a deal worse than your BATNA.

## STRATEGIC IMPLICATIONS

### How to Use Our BATNA in Negotiation

**1. Don't Reveal Desperation**
- ✅ DO: Act like we have other options (we do - 7/10 BATNA)
- ❌ DON'T: Say "you're our only lead" or "we really need this deal"
- ✅ DO: Mention "we're in conversations with other clients" (true - we could be)

**2. Confidently Hold Pricing**
- ✅ DO: Present pricing without apology
- ✅ DO: Emphasize ROI and value at every tier
- ❌ DON'T: Immediately discount when they push back
- ✅ DO: Offer structural alternatives (pilot, payment terms) not price cuts

**3. Be Willing to Walk**
- ✅ DO: Have clear walk-away criteria (<15M PYG)
- ✅ DO: Communicate walk-away professionally if hit
- ❌ DON'T: Threaten to walk (ultimatums backfire)
- ✅ DO: Make it their choice: "At that price point, it doesn't make sense for either of us. Let's explore if there's a structure that works."

**4. Leverage Their Weak BATNA**
- ✅ DO: Remind them of alternatives' costs and timelines
  - "Enterprise SaaS would be $80k over 3 years and take 3-6 months to implement"
  - "Building in-house would be $135k and 6-12 months, and you'd still need to maintain it"
  - "Your current process costs $364k per year and doesn't scale"
- ✅ DO: Emphasize our speed (2 weeks vs. 3-12 months)
- ✅ DO: Highlight our ROI (5,873% vs. alternatives)

### How to Respond If They Have a Strong Counter-BATNA

**If they say: "We have budget approved for [COMPETITOR]"**
→ Response: "That's great - it means you're serious about solving this. Let's compare what you'd get with [COMPETITOR] vs. what we deliver, including timeline and total cost of ownership."

**If they say: "We're already building this in-house"**
→ Response: "That's ambitious. Can I ask - what's your timeline and budget for that? Because we can have you live and analyzing feedback in 2 weeks, and you own the source code, so you're not locked into us."

**If they say: "We can wait / this isn't urgent"**
→ Response: "I understand. Just to clarify - how much is the current manual process costing you per month while you wait?" [Reframe: delay = cost]
