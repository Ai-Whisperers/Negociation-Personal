## 🎯 Negotiation Strategy Summary

### Core Approach: Reverse-Anchor

**Ask them first:**
> "What budget range were you considering for this solution?"

**Response Matrix:**
| Their Number | Our Response |
|--------------|--------------|
| ≥100M PYG | Accept + add value ✅ |
| 80-100M | Accept (target) ✅ |
| 60-80M | Work within range ✅ |
| 40-60M | Counteroffer or tier ⚠️ |
| 15-40M | Pilot or walk ⚠️ |
| <15M | Hard pass ❌ |

### Tactics

**1. ROI Bomb** - When they question price
> "You're losing $364,000/year to manual processes. Our solution costs $6,098. It pays for itself in less than a week."

**2. Comparison Wedge** - When they mention alternatives
> "Enterprise SaaS: $80k, 3-6 months. Build in-house: $135k, 12 months. Status quo: $24k/year forever. Us: $5.7k, 2 weeks, source code included."

**3. Champion Support** - Throughout
> "We'll provide executive summary, ROI calculator, and technical specs to help you present internally."

**4. Quick Win Frame** - Create urgency
> "Deploy by November 1st, show results in your year-end review."

**5. Concession Trade** - Never give without getting
> "I can do 60M instead of 80M if you provide a video testimonial and 2 referrals."

### Objection Responses

**"Too expensive"** → ROI: $357,902 saved (pays for itself in <1 week)
**"No track record"** → First client = competitive pricing + source code included
**"Build in-house"** → $499k total cost vs. $5.7k (26x faster)
**"Need to think"** → What concerns remain? (address them)
**"Can we get discount?"** → 90-98% cheaper already; trade for testimonial
