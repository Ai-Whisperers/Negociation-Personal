## 🎯 What is BATNA?

**BATNA = Best Alternative To a Negotiated Agreement**

This is your **Plan B** if the deal falls through. The better your BATNA, the more power you have in negotiation. If your BATNA is weak (you desperately need this deal), you have less leverage.
