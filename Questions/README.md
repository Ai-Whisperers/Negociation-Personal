## 💡 Tips for Answering

1. **Be Specific:** "We can deliver in 2 months" not "soon"
2. **Use Numbers:** "$15k minimum" not "around 15"
3. **Be Honest:** Admitting weaknesses helps prepare defenses
4. **Think Long-term:** Not just this deal, but relationship/reputation
5. **Update as Needed:** Come back and refine answers as you learn more
