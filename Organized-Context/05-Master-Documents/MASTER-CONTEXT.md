- 7 emotions (satisfaction, frustration, anger, trust, etc.)
- Churn risk (0-1 probability)
- Pain points (8+ categories)
- NPS classification (Promoter/Passive/Detractor)
- Sentiment score

**Output:**
- Real-time dashboard
- Professional Excel reports (multi-sheet, charts)
- CSV exports

- 10 seconds vs. 40 hours manual

- Result: 88% cheaper than traditional full-AI approach

### What They Get
- Production-ready v3.2.0 (not a prototype)
