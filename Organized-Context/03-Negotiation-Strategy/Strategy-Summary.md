## Core Strategy (The 3 Pillars)

### 1. Reverse-Anchor Approach
**Let them give the first price**

**Script:**
> "Before we discuss pricing, what budget range were you considering for this solution?"

**Why:**
- They might anchor HIGHER than our target (80M PYG)
- Shows confidence in our value
- We learn their budget reality
- Not constrained by low expectations

### 2. Pilot Model (Safety Net)
**If budget is tight, offer 10-day pilot**

**Pilot Structure:**
- Price: 20-30M PYG ($2,857-$4,286)
- Duration: 10 days
- Deliverable: 1 dataset analysis, dashboard, Excel export
- Upgrade path: Credit toward full tier if successful

### 3. Help José Luis Sell Internally
**He's our champion, not the decision-maker**

**Support Strategy:**
- Provide materials for CFO (ROI calculator)
- Provide materials for CTO (technical specs, security)
- Provide materials for Executives (strategic value)
- Make him the hero (quick wins he can show)

## Pricing Framework

### The Pricing Corridor

```
15M PYG ──────── 40M PYG ──────── 80M PYG ──────── 150M PYG
(Hard Pass)      (Minimum)        (Target)         (Stretch)
```

**Decision Matrix:**

| Their Anchor | Our Response | Strategy |
|--------------|--------------|----------|
| **≥100M PYG** | Accept + add value | "Perfect. Here's everything included." |
| **80-100M PYG** | Accept | "That aligns with our Large tier." |
| **60-80M PYG** | Work with it | "We can do Medium tier at this range." |
| **40-60M PYG** | Counteroffer | "We target 80M, but could do 60M with [pilot/terms/value]." |
| **15-40M PYG** | Pilot or walk | "That's below full package. 10-day pilot at that level?" |
| **<15M PYG** | **Hard pass** | "Below our minimum. We'd need 40M for full solution." |

### Tier Structure

**Small (S): 40M PYG ($5,714)**
- 1-2 datasets/month
- Basic volume
- 30 days support, 2 training sessions

**Medium (M): 60M PYG ($8,571)**
- 3-5 datasets/month
- Medium volume
- 60 days support, 3 training sessions
- Custom pain point categories

**Large (L): 80M PYG ($11,429)**
- 6-10 datasets/month
- High volume
- 90 days support, priority support
- Unlimited exports, custom branding

**Flat/Enterprise: 100-150M PYG ($14,286-$21,429)**
- Unlimited (with fair use caps)
- White-label option
- Dedicated support
- Custom features included

## Top 5 Objections & Responses

### 1. "That's too expensive"
**Response:**
> "Compared to the $364,000/year you're losing to manual processes and missed opportunities? Our $5,714 solution pays for itself in less than a week. It's not an expense - it's an investment with 5,873% ROI."

### 2. "You have no track record"
**Response:**
> "That's true - you'd be our first reference client. That's exactly why we're offering competitive pricing AND source code included. Many companies would charge 10x more and lock you in. With us, you get production-ready technology with zero vendor lock-in. And because you're first, we're highly motivated to make this a huge success."

### 3. "We can build this in-house"
**Response:**
> "You could. But that's $135,000 and 12 months. During that year, you'll lose another $364,000 to manual processes. Total cost: $499,000. We deploy in 2 weeks for $5,714. What's the opportunity cost of waiting?"

### 4. "Can we think about it?"
**Response:**
> "Of course. What specific concerns do you still have? I want to make sure we've addressed everything." [Then set specific follow-up date]

### 5. "Can we get a discount?"
**Response:**
> "We're already 90-98% cheaper than alternatives. However, if you can provide a video testimonial and 2 warm referrals, I could reduce to 60M instead of 80M. Would that work?"

## Never Concede

**Hard lines:**

1. ❌ **Below 15M PYG pricing** - Walk away
2. ❌ **Unlimited scope/support** - Unsustainable
3. ❌ **IP ownership** - They license, not own (until payment)
4. ❌ **24/7 support** - Impossible for 3-person team
5. ❌ **Free lifetime updates** - Kills future revenue

## Defense Tactics (Dirty Plays to Watch For)

### From "dirty plays from them to take care.md"

| Scenario | Signal | Counter-Measure |
|----------|--------|-----------------|
| **Delay payments** | Excuses, "admin issues" | Suspend access after 10 days late |
| **Copy product** | Request code, "local install" | Black-box build + limited license only |
| **Low-ball** | "Others do it cheaper" | Show cost breakdown + ROI |
| **Data fishing** | Request samples, model details | Anonymized demos only, no code/weights |
| **Contract tricks** | Last-minute changes | Re-read every line, require counter-signature |

**Protection:**
- Use NDA + SLA
- Deploy as black-box SaaS (during pilot)
- Source code only after full payment
- Log all usage (proof of ownership)
- Backups encrypted, off-site

## Concession Trading Rules

**Golden Rule:** Never give without getting

### If They Ask For... → We Want...

**10% discount →**
- Written testimonial + logo for website
- OR 2 referrals to potential clients
- OR case study rights with their brand

**Faster delivery →**
- Full payment upfront (not terms)
- OR 10% rush fee
- OR all materials same day

**More features →**
- Higher tier (Small → Medium)
- OR phased pricing (pay extra)
- OR extended contract (1 year vs. 60 days)

**Payment terms →**
- No discount (full price)
- OR guarantee of referral/case study
- OR signed contract with timeline

## Walk-Away Criteria

**When to walk away:**

1. ❌ Price <15M PYG ($2,143)
2. ❌ Rude/disrespectful behavior
3. ❌ Unreasonable demands (unlimited scope, 24/7 support, IP ownership)
4. ❌ Red flags (work "on spec," free work, payment concerns)
5. ❌ No testimonial/referral commitment + low price

**How to walk professionally:**
> "I appreciate your time, José Luis. Based on what we've discussed, I don't think we can find common ground right now. Our minimum to deliver quality is 40M PYG. If circumstances change, please reach out. Thank you."
