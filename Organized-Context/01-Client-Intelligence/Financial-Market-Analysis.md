## Executive Summary

Personal Paraguay (Núcleo S.A.) generated **$62 million in annual revenue (2024)** with **~1,429 employees**, operating as the #2 telecom in Paraguay. The company is **financially healthy** with strong margins, backed by Telecom Argentina (67.5% ownership). **Key Insight:** Strong financial position + parent company's digital transformation mandate = budget is likely NOT a constraint. Focus on strategic value, not just ROI.
