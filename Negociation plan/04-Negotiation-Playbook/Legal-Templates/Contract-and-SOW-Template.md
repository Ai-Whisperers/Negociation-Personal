## 9. TERM & TERMINATION

### 9.1 Term

This agreement begins on the Agreement Date and continues until:
- Project completion and acceptance
- End of included support period ([60-90] days post-launch)
- Earlier termination as provided below

### 9.2 Termination for Convenience

Either party may terminate this agreement with [30] days written notice to the other party, provided that:

**If Client terminates:**
- Client remains responsible for payment for all work completed to date
- Service Provider will deliver all work-in-progress in current state
- Client will pay pro-rata percentage of total fee based on milestones completed

**If Service Provider terminates:**
- Service Provider will deliver all work completed to date
- Client will pay only for milestones completed, not future milestones
- Service Provider will provide reasonable transition assistance (not to exceed [X] hours)

### 9.3 Termination for Cause

Either party may terminate immediately if:
- Other party materially breaches agreement and fails to cure within [15] days of written notice
- Other party becomes insolvent or files for bankruptcy
- Other party engages in fraud or illegal conduct related to agreement

**If terminated for Client's breach:**
- Client must pay all fees due through termination date
- Service Provider retains all payments received

**If terminated for Service Provider's breach:**
- Client may request refund of payments for uncompleted milestones
- Service Provider will deliver all completed work

### 9.4 Effects of Termination

Upon termination:
- All payment obligations for completed work become immediately due
- Service Provider will cease all work
- Each party will return other party's confidential information
- Sections 5 (IP), 6 (Confidentiality), 7 (Warranties), 8 (Liability), and 11 (General) survive termination
