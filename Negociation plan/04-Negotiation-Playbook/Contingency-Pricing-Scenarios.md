# Contingency Pricing Scenarios
**Purpose:** Prepared responses for alternative pricing requests and negotiation scenarios
**When to Use:** When client counters with different budget, payment structure, or scope requests
**Decision Authority:** See "Who Decides" for each scenario

---

## How to Use This Document

This document provides pre-calculated scenarios for common negotiation situations. Each scenario includes:
- **The Situation:** What the client requests
- **Your Response:** What to say immediately
- **The Math:** Financial impact for both parties
- **Decision Framework:** Accept, negotiate, or decline
- **Who Decides:** Authority boundaries (Kyrian vs escalate to Ivan)

**During negotiation:**
1. Listen to their counter-proposal
2. Find matching scenario below
3. Follow the response script
4. Check "Who Decides" before committing
5. Use "Buy Time" response if scenario not covered

## SCENARIO 1: Lower Budget Counter (35M PYG)

### The Situation:
José Luis says: *"We love the solution, but our budget is only 35M PYG. Can you work with that?"*

### Your Response:
> "I appreciate your honesty about budget. Let me see what we can do. At 35M, we'd need to adjust scope slightly. I can offer two options:
>
> **Option A:** Keep all features, but reduce training to 2 sessions instead of 3, and support period to 45 days instead of 60. Everything else stays the same.
>
> **Option B:** Do a **full pilot first for 25M PYG** - you get complete analysis of one dataset, full dashboard, and Excel export. If you love it, you pay the remaining 35M for full implementation (so 60M total, just split differently).
>
> Which approach feels better for your situation?"

### The Math:

**Option A: Reduced Support (35M PYG)**
| Component | Standard (60M) | Reduced (35M) | Cost Savings |
|-----------|----------------|---------------|--------------|
| Platform Development | ✅ Included | ✅ Included | $0 |
| All Features | ✅ Included | ✅ Included | $0 |
| Training Sessions | 3 sessions | 2 sessions | -$500 |
| Post-Launch Support | 60 days | 45 days | -$1,000 |
| **Total Discount** | - | - | **-$1,500** |

**Client Still Gets:**
- Complete platform with all automation features
- Source code ownership
- Daily/weekly reports
- Excel exports
- 2 comprehensive training sessions (enough for most teams)

**Option B: Pilot First (25M + 35M)**
- Same total cost (60M PYG)
- Just different payment structure
- Reduces risk for client
- Proves value before full commitment

### Decision Framework:

✅ **ACCEPT Option A if:**
- They genuinely can't go above 35M (verify)
- They agree to reduced training/support immediately
- Alternative is losing the deal

✅ **PUSH Option B first:**
- De-risks their decision
- Same total revenue for us
- Better psychological commitment (pilot proves value)

❌ **DON'T:**
- Go below 35M without Ivan's approval
- Reduce features (only reduce services)
- Offer monthly payment plans at this tier

### Who Decides:
**Kyrian:** Can approve Option A or B on the spot (within 35-60M range)
**Ivan:** Must approve anything below 35M or material feature changes

## SCENARIO 2: "Can We Pay Monthly?" Request

### The Situation:
José Luis says: *"We don't have 60M upfront. Can we pay monthly over 12 months?"*

### Your Response:
> "I understand cash flow concerns. Monthly payments are possible, but they require a different structure since we carry the implementation cost upfront. Here's what I can offer:
>
> **Monthly Payment Plan:**
> - **Total:** 72M PYG (vs 60M one-time) - the 12M difference covers our financing cost
> - **Term:** 12 monthly payments of 6M PYG
> - **Commitment:** Full 12-month contract (early termination requires paying remaining balance)
> - **Everything else stays the same:** All features, training, support included
>
> Alternatively, if you can do **40M upfront and 20M at 60 days**, I can keep it at 60M total. That's only 2 payments instead of 12 and saves you 12M PYG."

### The Math:

**Option A: 12 Monthly Payments**
| Item | Value | Calculation |
|------|-------|-------------|
| Base Price | 60M PYG | Original package |
| Financing Premium | +20% | Covers our cash flow impact |
| **Total Monthly Plan** | **72M PYG** | 12 × 6M PYG/month |
| Monthly Payment | 6M PYG | Affordable for most budgets |

**Option B: 40M + 20M (Recommended Counter)**
- **Payment 1:** 40M PYG at project start
- **Payment 2:** 20M PYG at 60 days (acceptance)
- **Total:** 60M PYG (no premium)
- **Benefit:** Saves them 12M vs monthly plan

**Why We Add 20% for Monthly:**
- We pay developers, hosting, API costs upfront
- 12 months of payment collection risk
- Administrative overhead (invoicing, tracking, collections)
- Standard industry practice for payment plans

### Decision Framework:

✅ **ACCEPT Monthly Plan if:**
- They genuinely can't pay upfront (verify)
- They agree to 20% premium (72M total)
- They sign contract with early termination clause

✅ **PUSH 40M+20M Option first:**
- Only 2 payments vs 12 (simpler)
- Saves them 12M PYG
- We get bulk of payment sooner

❌ **DON'T:**
- Offer monthly without premium (we lose money)
- Accept 3-6 months at 60M (too short for financing premium)
- Offer monthly below 72M without Ivan approval

### Who Decides:
**Kyrian:** Can approve monthly plan at 72M or 40M+20M at 60M
**Ivan:** Must approve monthly plans under 72M or custom terms

## SCENARIO 3: "We Want to Start Small" - Reduced Scope

### The Situation:
José Luis says: *"We're interested but want to start with just NPS classification and pain points. Can we add other features later?"*

### Your Response:
> "I appreciate you wanting to start focused. However, our platform is integrated - the churn prediction, emotions, and reporting all work together. Separating them actually **costs more** in the long run.
>
> Here's why: If we build it piecemeal, we'd charge:
> - **Phase 1** (NPS + Pain Points): 40M PYG
> - **Phase 2** (Add Churn + Emotions): 35M PYG
> - **Total:** 75M PYG
>
> Versus getting everything now for 60M PYG - you **save 15M** and have full functionality from day one.
>
> If budget timing is the issue, what about the **pilot option** (25M PYG)? You get *everything* for one dataset, see the full value, then decide on full implementation."

### The Math:

**Piecemeal Approach (Higher Total Cost):**
| Phase | Features | Cost | Why More Expensive |
|-------|----------|------|-------------------|
| Phase 1 | NPS + Pain Points | 40M PYG | Full dev environment, training, deployment |
| Phase 2 | Add Churn + Emotions | 35M PYG | Re-deployment, additional training, integration testing |
| **Total** | Everything | **75M PYG** | **+15M vs buying all upfront** |

**Full Package Upfront (Recommended):**
| Package | Features | Cost | Savings |
|---------|----------|------|---------|
| Complete | Everything | 60M PYG | **-15M vs phased** |

**Why Phased Costs More:**
- Duplicate deployment effort
- Additional training sessions
- Integration complexity
- Re-testing entire platform
- More project management overhead

**Pilot Alternative (Best of Both Worlds):**
- **Cost:** 25M PYG
- **Gets:** Everything (all features) for 1 dataset
- **Then:** Pay 35M more for full deployment (60M total)
- **Benefit:** See full value before committing, same total cost

### Decision Framework:

❌ **DON'T Accept Reduced Scope:**
- Reduces value proposition (they won't see full ROI)
- Creates more work for us (phased deployment)
- Opens door to "good enough" and not buying Phase 2

✅ **REDIRECT to Pilot:**
- Same risk reduction they're seeking
- They see ALL features
- Better demonstrates value
- Same revenue for us (60M total)

✅ **IF THEY INSIST on Phase 1 only:**
- Quote 40M PYG (not 30M)
- Make clear Phase 2 will cost additional 35M
- Get Ivan's approval before committing

### Who Decides:
**Kyrian:** Can redirect to pilot, cannot accept reduced scope without Ivan
**Ivan:** Must approve any reduced-scope packages or phased approaches

### The Math:

**Why We Don't Offer Free Trials:**

| Our Cost to Deliver "Free Trial" | Amount |
|-----------------------------------|--------|
| Developer time (setup, config) | ~$2,000 |
| Infrastructure setup | ~$200 |
| OpenAI API tokens | ~$50 |
| Training/support time | ~$500 |
| **Total Cost to Us** | **~$2,750** |

**If they don't buy → We lose $2,750 + opportunity cost**

**Pilot Alternative (Low-Risk, Not Free):**

| Item | Details |
|------|---------|
| **Investment** | 25M PYG ($3,571) |

### Decision Framework:

- We lose money on non-buyers

- Same de-risking benefit for them

✅ **Frame Pilot as "Best of Both Worlds":**
- De-risks their decision (like a trial)

- Low investment ($3,571)
- Credited toward full package
