## Key Meeting Mantras

### Before Meeting
- "We are prepared, confident, and have strong leverage" (7/10 BATNA)
- "We want the reference but CAN walk away if needed"
- "Our job: Make José Luis a hero who can sell internally"

### During Meeting
- "What budget range are you considering?" (reverse anchor FIRST)
- "Let them talk 70%, we talk 30%" (listen, don't over-talk)
- "Never concede without getting something back" (trade concessions)
- "Focus on value and ROI, not just features" (sell outcomes)

### Pricing Discussion
- "$364k/year pain → $5-11k solution = 97% savings, 5,873% ROI"
- "Production-ready NOW means you deploy before Q4 ends"
- "We can structure this as Pilot (20-30M) or Full (40-80M)"
- "What ROI numbers would help you sell to CFO/CTO?"

### If Challenged
- "We understand. Let's explore options together" (collaboration)
- "What would make this work for you?" (reverse objection)
- "Based on your $364k pain, this pays for itself in <2 weeks" (ROI anchor)
- "We'd love to work together, but 40M is our minimum" (firm boundary)

## Success Metrics

### Minimum Success (Walk Away Happy)
- ✅ Deal at 40M PYG or higher
- ✅ Clear scope definition
- ✅ Reasonable timeline (1-2 months)
- ✅ Acceptable payment terms

### Good Success
- ✅ Deal at 60-80M PYG (target range)
- ✅ Client reference commitment
- ✅ Clear next steps with deadlines
- ✅ Positive relationship established

### Exceptional Success
- ✅ Deal at 100M+ PYG (stretch goal)
- ✅ Multi-year partnership discussion
- ✅ Referral opportunity identified
- ✅ Upsell potential recognized

### Walk Away If
- ❌ Price below 40M without justification
- ❌ Unlimited scope demands
- ❌ Unreasonable timeline (<3 weeks)
- ❌ Payment red flags
- ❌ Disrespectful or bad faith behavior
