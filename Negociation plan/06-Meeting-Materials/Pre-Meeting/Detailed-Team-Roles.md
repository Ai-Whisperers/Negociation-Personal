### Decision Trees for Common Scenarios

#### Scenario 1: They anchor at 50M PYG
**Kyrian's response:**
1. Pause (3 seconds - show you're considering)
2. "We were targeting our Medium tier at 60M, but I think we can work in that range."
3. "Let me show you what's included in the Medium package at 60M..."
4. Negotiate up to 60M or accept 50M with reduced scope (Small tier)
5. Add value-wins: extra training, extended support

#### Scenario 2: They say "too expensive"
**Kyrian's response:**
1. Don't get defensive
2. "I appreciate that concern. Can I ask - compared to what?"
3. Listen to their answer
4. Pivot to ROI: "Let's look at the cost of your current process..."
5. Show ROI calculator: $357k saved vs. $5.7k cost
6. Reframe: "So it pays for itself in less than a week."
7. If still concerned: Offer pilot model

#### Scenario 3: They ask for time to think
**Kyrian's response:**
1. "Of course, this is an important decision."
2. "Can I ask - what specific concerns or questions do you still have?"
3. Address those concerns directly
4. "What timeline works for you to make a decision?"
5. Set specific follow-up date
6. "What materials would help with your internal review?"
7. Offer stakeholder materials

#### Scenario 4: They bring up competitors
**Kyrian's response:**
1. Don't badmouth competitors
2. "That makes sense - you should evaluate all options."
3. "Which solutions are you considering?"
4. Pull out comparison matrix
5. Emphasize unique advantages (speed, source code, cost)
6. "Would a pilot help prove our value quickly?"

#### Scenario 5: Price too low (<40M PYG)
**Kyrian's response:**
1. Don't immediately reject
2. "I understand budget constraints."
3. "At that price point, we could offer a 10-day pilot..."
4. Explain pilot model (20-30M range)
5. "This lets you see results with your data, then we can discuss the full package."
6. If they insist on <15M for full package: Walk away gracefully
