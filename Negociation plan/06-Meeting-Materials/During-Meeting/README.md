### During the Meeting (60 minutes)

**Ivan's Primary Responsibilities:**
1. **meeting-notes-live.md** - Capture everything chronologically
2. **questions-asked.md** - Log each question as it's asked
3. **commitments-made.md** - Note all promises in real-time
4. **objections-raised.md** - Document concerns as they arise
5. **pricing-discussion-notes.md** - Detailed pricing negotiation tracking (Kyrian helps here)

**Note-Taking Best Practices:**
- Write exact quotes when possible (use "quotation marks")
- Note timestamps for each major section
- Flag critical moments with stars ⭐ or highlights
- Use shorthand but be clear enough to understand later
- Don't try to be perfect - capture the essence
- If you miss something, ask team after meeting

**Team Support for Ivan:**
- Kyrian and Jonathan should slow down if Ivan signals
- If pricing gets intense, Kyrian helps with `pricing-discussion-notes.md`
- Take quick note-taking breaks between sections (30 seconds)

## Tips for Effective Note-Taking

### For Ivan (Primary Note-Taker)

**Before Meeting:**
- Review all 5 templates so you know where to capture what
- Have printed copies in front of you
- Set up dual monitors if possible (one for notes, one for meeting)
- Test typing/writing speed - use what's fastest for you

**During Meeting:**
- Don't try to write everything - capture key points
- Use abbreviations (JL = José Luis, PP = Personal Paraguay, PYG = Guaraní)
- Flag items for team discussion with ⭐
- If you miss something, put [MISSED] and ask team after
- Focus on WHAT was said more than HOW (unless tone matters)
- Timestamp major sections
- Note when someone new joins or leaves the meeting

**Managing Multiple Documents:**
- Start with `meeting-notes-live.md` as master
- When questions asked → note in master + log in `questions-asked.md`
- When commitments made → note in master + log in `commitments-made.md`
- When objections raised → note in master + log in `objections-raised.md`
- During pricing → focus heavily on `pricing-discussion-notes.md` (Kyrian helps here)

**If You Get Overwhelmed:**
- Signal to Kyrian/Jonathan to slow down
- Focus on master notes first
- Fill in specialized documents during section breaks or after meeting
- Don't sacrifice listening for writing - prioritize understanding

---

### For Kyrian & Jonathan

**Support Ivan:**
- Watch for Ivan's signals to slow down
- Speak clearly and don't rush
- Repeat important numbers/dates twice
- Take brief pauses between major points
- After making a commitment, confirm: "Ivan, did you get that?"

**Your Own Notes:**
- Take your own quick notes as backup
- Especially during your sections (Kyrian: pricing, Jonathan: demo)
- Note client reactions you observe
- Flag anything you think Ivan might have missed
