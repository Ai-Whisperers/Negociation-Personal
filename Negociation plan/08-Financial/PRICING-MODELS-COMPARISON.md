## Quick Decision Guide

**Answer these 3 questions first:**

1. **Does Personal Paraguay need significant custom development?**
   - ✅ YES → Consider Model 1 (Premium Development: 75-80M upfront)
   - ❌ NO → Consider Model 2 or 4 (Hybrid or Annual)

2. **What's Personal Paraguay's likely budget?**
   - 150M+ available → Model 1 works
   - 100-150M available → Model 2 or 3
   - 80-100M available → Model 4 or small version of Model 2
   - <80M available → Need to offer pilot or walk away

3. **What's your business goal?**
   - Maximize per-client revenue → Model 1 or 3
   - Balance revenue and scalability → Model 2
   - Fast close, move on → Model 4

## Pricing Psychology: How to Frame Each Model

### **Frame Model 1 (Premium Dev) as:**
> "We'll build a custom solution tailored exactly to Personal Paraguay's needs, with dedicated development time. This includes full integration with your existing systems and white-glove support. Think of the 77.5M as an investment in a custom-built tool that's yours forever, with ongoing optimization for just 6M/month."

**When to use:** If they mention needing customization, integration, or "we're unique."

---

### **Frame Model 2 (Hybrid) as:** ⭐ RECOMMENDED
> "We've built a production-ready solution that we can deploy in 2-3 weeks. The 45M covers professional setup, training, and migration. Then it's just 5M/month for hosting, support, and continuous improvements. You're getting enterprise software at a fraction of the cost."

**When to use:** If they want quick deployment and proven solution (your repo positioning).

---

### **Frame Model 3 (Pure Subscription) as:**
> "Zero upfront investment. Start using the software immediately for just 11M/month, which includes everything—setup, hosting, support, improvements, everything. It's like Netflix for customer feedback analytics: simple monthly subscription, cancel anytime after 12 months."

**When to use:** If they balk at upfront costs or say "we don't have budget until next quarter."

---

### **Frame Model 4 (Annual SaaS) as:**
> "Simple annual license, just like Microsoft Office or Salesforce. 70M per year (or 7M/month) and you get everything: software, hosting, support, updates. No hidden fees, no surprises. Deploy in 2 weeks, start saving immediately."

**When to use:** If they want simplicity and fast decision (least friction to close).

## Red Flags & Walk-Away Scenarios

**Do NOT accept deal if:**
- ❌ Total Year 1 revenue <40M PYG (unless it's a strategic pilot)
- ❌ Monthly fee <3M PYG (can't be profitable)
- ❌ Client expects "unlimited improvements" for fixed fee
- ❌ Client won't commit to minimum 12 months
- ❌ Payment terms >90 days (cash flow risk)
- ❌ Client wants source code ownership (you lose scalability)

**Consider walking away and offering alternatives:**
- Pilot program (3 months, 20-30M PYG, proof of concept)
- Referral to cheaper alternative
- Revisit in 6 months when they have budget
