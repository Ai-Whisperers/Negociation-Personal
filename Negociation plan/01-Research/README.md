## Internal Analysis Files (1 file)

### 1. [BATNA-Analysis.md](Internal-Analysis/BATNA-Analysis.md)
**Purpose:** Assessment of alternatives (ours and theirs) to determine negotiation leverage

**Our BATNA: 7/10 (STRONG POSITION)**

**Our Alternatives:**
1. ✅ **Sell to different telecom clients** (several in pipeline)
2. ✅ **Keep product as portfolio piece** (already built, production-ready)
3. ✅ **Use for own consulting work** (demonstrate capabilities to prospects)
4. ✅ **License to multiple smaller clients** (SaaS recurring revenue model)
5. ✅ **No sunk cost pressure** (product already completed, no rush)

**Why Our BATNA is Strong:**
- Product is DONE (no development risk or cost)
- Multiple potential buyers (not dependent on Personal Paraguay)
- Can walk away without loss (portfolio value alone is high)
- No financial pressure to close (company is stable)
- Time is on our side (product isn't degrading or becoming obsolete)

**Their BATNA: 2-3/10 (WEAK POSITION)**

**Their Alternatives:**
1. ❌ **Continue manual analysis** - $364k/year cost, 40 hrs/month, slow, error-prone
2. ❌ **Build in-house solution** - 6-12 months, $100k+ development cost, high risk, requires AI expertise
3. ❌ **Generic sentiment tools** - Don't solve churn prediction, Spanish support limited
4. ❌ **Big vendor solutions** - Expensive ($50k-$200k), long implementation (6+ months), overkill
5. ❌ **Wait and hope problem goes away** - Executive pressure won't allow this

**Why Their BATNA is Weak:**
- Every alternative is worse than our solution (cost, time, or quality)
- Executive pressure to solve NOW (time pressure)
- José Luis personally motivated (career pressure)
- Manual process is unsustainable (operational pressure)
- No good off-the-shelf alternatives exist (market gap)

**Power Dynamic: WE HAVE LEVERAGE**
- Our BATNA (7/10) >> Their BATNA (2-3/10)
- We can walk away comfortably, they lose if we walk
- They need us more than we need them
- BUT: We want Personal Paraguay as reference client (telecom sector credibility)

**Negotiation Implications:**
- ✅ Confidently maintain minimum price (40M PYG)
- ✅ Don't over-negotiate or appear desperate
- ✅ Walk away if terms are unfavorable
- ✅ Focus on value (ROI), not just price
- ⚠️ Balance confidence with relationship-building (we want the reference)
- ⚠️ Don't be arrogant (collaboration > confrontation)

**Walk-Away Criteria:**
- Price below 40M PYG without strong justification
- Unlimited scope changes without compensation
- Unreasonable timeline demands (<3 weeks)
- Payment terms that create cash flow risk
- Client red flags (payment history issues, legal problems)
- Disrespectful behavior or bad faith negotiation
