## Current Situation & Pain Points

### Primary Problem
**Manual customer feedback analysis** is time-consuming, labor-intensive, and doesn't scale.

**Evidence:**
- José Luis's background as Survey Coordinator (he knows this pain personally)
- Current role requires analyzing customer feedback to improve NPS and reduce churn
- Manual analysis limits:
  - Volume: Can only process ~100-200 comments manually in reasonable time
  - Speed: Takes hours/days vs. real-time insights
  - Insights: Human bias, fatigue, can't detect patterns across thousands of comments
  - Scalability: Can't handle peak periods (promotional campaigns, service issues)

### Specific Pain Point Trigger
**Answer from Updated Context:**
- They want to **analyze all customer feedback automatically instead of manually**
- **Key motivation:** Eliminate manual review process
- **Value proposition focus:** Time savings + automation + faster insights

### What's It Costing Them Now?

**Time Cost:**
- Estimated 40 hours/month for manual feedback analysis
- At $50/hour labor cost = **$2,000/month = $24,000/year**

**Opportunity Cost:**
- **Missed churn signals:** Can't analyze all feedback, so miss at-risk customers
- **Wrong product decisions:** Build features based on gut feeling, not data
- **Competitive disadvantage:** Competitors using AI insights will outpace them
- **Slow response time:** Takes weeks to compile feedback reports vs. real-time

**Hidden Costs:**
- Analyst fatigue and burnout
- Human bias in analysis
- Can't scale beyond ~100 comments (telecoms likely get thousands/month)
- No structured pain point categorization
- No churn risk prediction
- No emotion analysis
