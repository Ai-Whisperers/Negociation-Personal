# 14-Day Post-Meeting Follow-Up Strategy

**Date:** 2025-10-16
**Meeting Date:** October 17, 2025
**Purpose:** Maintain momentum and close deal within 14 days if they don't commit immediately

---

## Overview: The Follow-Up Framework

**Goal:** Convert "I need to think about it" into signed contract within 14 days

**Key Principle:** Stay helpful, not pushy. Provide value at each touchpoint.

**Success Metrics:**
- ✅ Contract signed within 7-14 days
- ✅ OR Pilot commitment within 7 days
- ✅ OR Clear "no" so we can move on

## FOLLOW-UP PRINCIPLES

### DO:
- ✅ Provide value at each touchpoint (articles, case studies, materials)
- ✅ Be helpful, not pushy
- ✅ Respect their time and process
- ✅ Create gentle urgency (deadlines, bonuses)
- ✅ Make it easy to say yes (simple contracts, flexible terms)
- ✅ Ask direct questions when needed
- ✅ Exit professionally if it's dead

### DON'T:
- ❌ Send generic "just checking in" emails
- ❌ Be pushy or aggressive
- ❌ Email more than once per day (except deadline day)
- ❌ Guilt trip or manipulate
- ❌ Give up after 1-2 emails (follow up 10-14 days)
- ❌ Burn bridges if they say no
- ❌ Follow up forever (14 days max, then exit)

---

## PSYCHOLOGICAL TACTICS

### Reciprocity
**Provide value first, ask second:**
- Day 1: Send materials (help them)
- Day 4: Send article (educate them)
- Day 6: Send case study (inspire them)
- THEN ask for commitment

### Scarcity
**Use deadlines strategically:**
- "Bonus package expires Friday Oct 24"
- "We only take 2-3 clients per quarter"
- "Priority deployment available this week only"

### Social Proof
**Show others are doing this:**
- "Similar telecom companies are using AI to reduce churn by 15-25%"
- "Companies like yours typically choose Medium tier"
- "Most CX leaders are moving to AI-powered analysis"

### Authority
**Position as expert:**
- Share industry articles/research
- Provide data-driven insights
- Offer to educate their stakeholders

### Commitment & Consistency
**Get small yeses first:**
- "Can I send you the materials?" (Yes)
- "Would a pilot make sense?" (Yes)
- "Can we schedule a call?" (Yes)
- Leading to: "Can we move forward?" (Yes)
