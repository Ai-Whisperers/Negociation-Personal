## 🎯 QUICK DECISION TREE: Pricing Response (30 Seconds)

**Use this when José Luis gives a budget number:**

```
┌─ THEY SAY: "X million PYG"
│
├─ X ≥ 100M PYG
│  └─→ ✅ ACCEPT IMMEDIATELY
│      "Perfect. That works for our Large tier with full features."
│      → Go to: MAXIMIZE VALUE (add 20-50M in extras)
│
├─ 80M ≤ X < 100M
│  └─→ ✅ ACCEPT + POSITION
│      "That aligns with our Large tier. Let me show what's included."
│      → Go to: LARGE TIER PITCH
│
├─ 60M ≤ X < 80M
│  └─→ ✅ ACCEPT + RECOMMEND UPGRADE
│      "That fits our Medium tier perfectly."
│      → Go to: MEDIUM TIER PITCH (upsell to Large)
│
├─ 40M ≤ X < 60M
│  └─→ ⚠️ ACCEPT BUT NUDGE UP
│      "We can work in that range. Small tier at 40M or Medium at 60M."
│      → Go to: VALUE GAP STRATEGY (show why Medium is worth 20M more)
│
├─ 20M ≤ X < 40M
│  └─→ ⚠️ PILOT OR NEGOTIATE UP
│      "That's below our full package. Let's discuss a pilot at 25-30M."
│      → Go to: PILOT PITCH (prove value first)
│
├─ 15M ≤ X < 20M
│  └─→ ❌ ULTRA-LIGHT PILOT OR WALK
│      "Our minimum is 40M for full package, 25M for pilot."
│      → Go to: FINAL OFFER (20M ultra-light) or WALK AWAY
│
└─ X < 15M
   └─→ ❌ HARD PASS - WALK AWAY
       "That's below our minimum cost. We'd need 40M minimum."
       → Go to: WALK-AWAY SCRIPT
```

## 💡 VALUE GAP STRATEGY

**Use when they're at 40-50M and you want to push to 60M:**

### The Formula

```
Current Budget: 40-50M PYG
Target: 60M PYG (Medium Tier)
Gap: 10-20M PYG to close

STRATEGY: Show what they LOSE by choosing Small over Medium
```

### The Pitch

**Step 1: Acknowledge their budget**
> "I understand 40-50M is your target. At 40M, you get our Small tier - solid core features."

**Step 2: Show the limitation**
> "The limitation at Small tier is: no automated daily reports, limited volume (1-2 datasets/month), only 30 days support."

**Step 3: Remind them of their #1 value**
> "Your #1 need is automation - specifically, eliminating those 40 hours/month of manual work. Small tier reduces it by 90%, but Medium tier eliminates it by 99.9% because of automated daily reports."

**Step 4: Quantify the gap**
> "The difference between 90% savings and 99.9% savings is 4 hours/month of manual work. That's $500/month × 12 months = **$6,000/year you're still losing** by going with Small tier."

**Step 5: Show the ROI of the upgrade**
> "Upgrading from Small (40M) to Medium (60M) costs an extra **20M PYG = $2,857**. But it saves you an extra **$6,000/year**. That's 210% ROI on the upgrade alone. It pays for itself in 5.7 months."

**Step 6: Make the ask**
> "For less than $3,000 more, you get:
> - Automated daily reports (your #1 value)
> - 2x the support (60 days vs 30)
> - 2x the volume (3-5 datasets vs 1-2)
> - API access for future integration
>
> Doesn't it make sense to invest the extra $2,857 to get the full automation you're looking for?"

**Step 7: Sweeten with bonuses**
> "And if you commit to Medium tier at 60M by end of this week, I'll include:
> - 4th training session (10M value)
> - Extended support to 75 days
> - Priority deployment (10 days instead of 14)
>
> That's 23M in extra value for free. Deal?"

## 🎁 CONCESSION SEQUENCING

**When to give what:**

### Round 1: First Objection (Price)
**They say:** "Can you do better on price?"

**You give:**
- Payment flexibility: 50/30/20 over 60 days
- "This helps your cash flow without changing the value."

**Why:** Low cost to you, high perceived value, doesn't discount

---

### Round 2: Second Objection (Budget constraints)
**They say:** "We really need it lower."

**You give:**
- Extended support: 60 days → 75 days
- 3rd training session
- "I can add these to sweeten the deal."

**Why:** Still low cost, builds goodwill, shows flexibility

---

### Round 3: Third Objection (Need more to justify internally)
**They say:** "I need more to show my CFO."

**You give:**
- Priority deployment (10 days vs 14)
- Custom pain point categories
- ROI calculator + CFO 1-pager
- "Let's make your CFO presentation bulletproof."

**Why:** Helps them sell internally, positions you as partner

---

### Round 4: Final Push (They're still hesitant)
**They say:** "We need one more thing..."

**You get first:**
> "I can make one final concession, but I need something in return. If you commit to this deal by Friday (Oct 24) AND provide a video testimonial after 60 days, I can..."

**Then you give:**
- Price reduction: 80M → 70M (10M discount)
- OR Tier upgrade: Medium price, Large features
- OR Extended partnership: 6-month support at 60-day price

**Why:** Never give without getting, testimonial worth more than discount

---

### STOP: Never Go Beyond Round 4

**If they're still negotiating after 4 rounds:**
- They're either not serious, or budget is truly not there
- Options:
  1. Offer pilot (25M) to prove value
  2. Walk away professionally
  3. Suggest revisiting in 30-60 days when budget cycle changes

## 🕐 TIMING STRATEGY

**When to use each tactic during the 60-minute meeting:**

### Minutes 0-15: Discovery & Demo
- **Don't mention price yet**
- Focus on: Pain, value, automation
- Goal: Establish need and desire

### Minutes 15-25: Value & ROI
- **Still don't mention price**
- Focus on: $364k/year cost, ROI, alternatives
- Goal: Frame value before price

### Minutes 25-40: PRICING DISCUSSION
- **NOW ask for their budget**
- "What budget range are you considering?"
- Use decision tree based on their response
- Goal: Get to price agreement

### Minutes 40-50: Objection Handling
- Use objection → response trees
- Sequential concessions (Rounds 1-4)
- Goal: Overcome final barriers

### Minutes 50-60: Close
- Summarize agreement
- Set next steps (contract signing, kickoff)
- Get commitment (verbal or signed)
- Goal: Lock in the deal

## 🎯 SUCCESS METRICS TRACKER

**Check these during negotiation:**

### Green Flags (Deal is progressing well)
- ✅ They gave budget number ≥40M PYG
- ✅ They're asking "how" questions (not "why")
- ✅ They're discussing internal approval process
- ✅ They're asking about implementation timeline
- ✅ They mention other stakeholders positively ("CFO will love this ROI")

### Yellow Flags (Proceed with caution)
- ⚠️ Budget <40M PYG (offer pilot)
- ⚠️ Vague timeline ("maybe this quarter")
- ⚠️ Comparing to multiple competitors
- ⚠️ Asking for "just one more discount"
- ⚠️ Avoiding commitment ("we need to think about it")

### Red Flags (Consider walking away)
- 🚩 Budget <15M and won't do pilot
- 🚩 Unrealistic demands (unlimited scope, 24/7 support)
- 🚩 Payment concerns or credit issues
- 🚩 Wants to "try before buying" (free work)
- 🚩 Rude, disrespectful, bad faith behavior
- 🚩 Timeline <1 week (impossible)

## 🔥 EMERGENCY SCRIPTS

**Use these when stuck:**

### "I need to think about it" → Force Decision
> "I understand. Can I ask: on a scale of 1-10, how likely are you to move forward if we address your concerns?"
>
> [If 7+]: "Great! What specific concerns can I address right now?"
> [If 4-6]: "Let's do a pilot - 25M PYG, 10 days, prove the value first."
> [If 1-3]: "Let's reconnect when this becomes a priority. I'll follow up in 60 days."

### "We're going with a competitor" → Last Attempt
> "Can I ask which competitor? I want to make sure you're comparing full value - deployment time, total cost of ownership, and vendor lock-in. If they're truly better for you, I'll respect that. But if there's a gap in your evaluation, I'd like a chance to address it."

### "Your price is 2x what we budgeted" → Reframe or Exit
> "I appreciate the transparency. Our pricing is based on the $364,000/year value we deliver. What we can do:
> 1. **Pilot at 25M** - Prove value first, upgrade later
> 2. **Phased approach** - Small tier now (40M), upgrade when budget available
> 3. **Reconnect next quarter** - When budget is approved
>
> Which makes most sense?"
