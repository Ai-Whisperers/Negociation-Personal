## What's Inside the Playbook

### Quick Reference Card
- **Pricing Tiers:** S/M/L structure with exact numbers
- **Reverse Anchor Script:** "What budget range are you considering?"
- **ROI One-Liner:** "$364k pain → $5-11k solution = 97% savings, 5,873% ROI"
- **Walk-Away Line:** "We'd love to work together, but 40M PYG is our minimum"

### Section I: Strategic Foundation
- **Power Dynamics:** Our BATNA 7/10 vs. their BATNA 2-3/10
- **Collaboration Approach:** Win-win mindset (we want the reference)
- **Core Philosophy:** Value-based pricing, not cost-plus

### Section II: Pricing Strategy
- **Pricing Corridor:**
  - Hard Pass: <40M PYG (walk away)
  - Minimum: 40M PYG ($5,714 USD)
  - Target: 60-80M PYG ($8,571-$11,429 USD)
  - Stretch: 100-150M PYG ($14,286-$21,429 USD)

- **Anchoring Strategy:**
  - Primary: REVERSE ANCHOR (let them bid first)
  - Fallback: Anchor at 80M PYG if forced to bid first

- **Pricing Tiers:**
  - **Tier S (Small):** 40M PYG - Basic deployment
  - **Tier M (Medium):** 60M PYG - Standard deployment + support
  - **Tier L (Large):** 80M PYG - Premium deployment + extended support

### Section III: Deal Structures
- **Full Package Options:** Tier S/M/L with different service levels
- **Pilot Model:** 20-30M PYG for 10-day trial (de-risk option)
- **Payment Terms:** Upfront, milestone-based (50/50), or monthly options

### Section IV: Negotiation Tactics (The Big 5)
1. **ROI Bomb:** "$364k/year pain → $5-11k solution = <2 weeks payback"
2. **Comparison Wedge:** "Building in-house would cost $100k+ and take 6-12 months"
3. **Champion Support:** "José Luis, what ROI numbers would help you sell to CFO?"
4. **Quick Win Frame:** "Production-ready NOW means you can deploy before Q4 ends"
5. **Concession Trade:** "We can add training sessions if you can approve 60M instead of 40M"

### Section V: Objection Handling
**Top 5 Objections with Responses:**

1. **"Budget is limited / Can we go lower?"**
   - Response: ROI justification ($364k pain), pilot option (20-30M)

2. **"Timeline is too fast / We need more time"**
   - Response: Pilot option, phased rollout, Q4 urgency alignment

3. **"What if it doesn't work?"**
   - Response: Production v3.2.0 (99%+ uptime), pilot de-risks, black-box deployment (no IT burden)

4. **"We're evaluating other options"**
   - Response: "Great! How do those alternatives compare on time-to-value and cost?" (none can compete)

5. **"We need to build internal consensus"**
   - Response: "Let's prepare materials together - what ROI numbers would help?" (arm José Luis)

### Section VI: Concession Strategy
**Easy Concessions (Low cost, high perceived value):**
- ✅ Additional training sessions (2-3 hours)
- ✅ Extended support period (3→6 months)
- ✅ Custom pain point categories (30 min config)
- ✅ Automated email reports (1-2 days setup)
- ✅ Priority support response time

**NEVER Concede:**
- ❌ Below 40M PYG minimum
- ❌ Unlimited scope changes
- ❌ Source code access before payment
- ❌ 24/7 support
- ❌ Liability for business decisions

### Section VII: Black-Box Deployment Strategy
- **Why:** Protects IP, prevents scope creep, reduces support burden
- **How:** Hosted solution, API access only, no source code
- **Benefits for Client:** No IT maintenance, guaranteed uptime, seamless updates
- **Benefits for Us:** IP protection, controlled environment, easier support

### Section VIII: Meeting Flow Strategy
**50-Minute Structured Agenda:**
1. Opening (5 min) - Rapport, agenda confirmation
2. Discovery (10 min) - Understand current pain (let them talk 70%)
3. Demo (15 min) - Show value (automation, reports, ease of use)
4. Pricing (15 min) - Reverse anchor, present options, handle objections
5. Q&A (10 min) - Address concerns calmly
6. Close (5 min) - Concrete next steps with deadlines

### Section IX: Walk-Away Criteria
**RED FLAGS - Walk Away If:**
- Price below 40M PYG without strong justification
- Unlimited scope demands without compensation
- Unreasonable timeline (<3 weeks implementation)
- Payment issues or credit red flags
- Disrespectful or bad faith behavior
- Legal liability for client's business decisions

### Section X: Success Metrics
- **Minimum Success:** 40M PYG, clear scope, reasonable timeline
- **Good Success:** 60-80M PYG, client reference commitment
- **Exceptional Success:** 100M+ PYG, multi-year partnership potential

### Section XI: Team Roles & Coordination
- **Kyrian:** Business lead, pricing discussions, closing
- **Jonathan:** Technical demo, architecture questions
- **Ivan:** Note-taking, strategic advisor, bad cop if needed
- **Communication Signals:** Pre-agreed hand signals for team coordination

### Section XII: Contingency Plans
- **If they push below 40M:** Pilot option (20-30M for 10 days)
- **If they want more time:** Phased approach, pilot first
- **If they compare to competitors:** Highlight time-to-value and production-ready status
- **If CFO/CTO join unexpectedly:** Pivot to ROI (CFO) or technical specs (CTO)
- **If they need to walk away:** Professional close, leave door open, follow-up in 1 week

### Section XIII: Key Mantras
- "What budget range are you considering?" (reverse anchor)
- "We want the reference but CAN walk away"
- "Let them talk 70%, we talk 30%"
- "Never concede without getting something back"
- "Focus on value and ROI, not just features"

## How to Use the Playbook

### 24 Hours Before Meeting
**MUST READ (30 minutes):**
- Read the ENTIRE playbook cover to cover
- Memorize pricing boundaries (40M minimum, 60-80M target, 150M stretch)
- Memorize reverse anchor script: "What budget range are you considering?"
- Memorize top 5 objection responses
- Review walk-away criteria

**PRACTICE (15 minutes):**
- Role-play pricing discussion with team
- Practice reverse anchor script
- Practice objection responses
- Practice concession trades

### Morning of Meeting
**QUICK REVIEW (10 minutes):**
- Re-read Quick Reference Card (Section 0)
- Review pricing tiers and concessions
- Review team roles and communication signals
- Mental prep: Remember leverage (7/10 BATNA)

### During Meeting
**HAVE OPEN:**
- Quick Reference Card (Section 0) - visible on laptop
- Objection Handling (Section V) - ready to reference
- Meeting Flow Strategy (Section VIII) - time management

**KEY REMINDERS:**
- Let them bid first (reverse anchor)
- Listen 70%, talk 30%
- Never concede without getting something back
- Focus on ROI ($364k pain → $5-11k solution)
- Stay calm, confident, collaborative

### After Meeting
**IMMEDIATE DEBRIEF:**
- What worked? What didn't?
- What objections came up that we didn't anticipate?
- What concessions did we make?
- What did we learn about their position?
- Update playbook with lessons learned
