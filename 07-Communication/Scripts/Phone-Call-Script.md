## Script #1: Pre-Meeting Confirmation Call

**Timing:** Day before meeting (Oct 16, 2025)
**Duration:** 5 minutes
**Caller:** Kyrian
**Goal:** Confirm attendance, set expectations, build rapport

### Opening

```
"Hi José Luis, this is Kyrian from AI Whisperers.

I wanted to reach out quickly to confirm our meeting tomorrow at [TIME].
Is that still good for you?"

[Wait for confirmation]

"Perfect. Just to set expectations - we'll have about an hour together.
Jonathan will walk through a live demo of the platform, and then we'll
discuss how this fits your needs and the investment details.

Do you have any questions before tomorrow?"

[Address any questions]

"Great. One quick question - will anyone else from Personal be joining us?
Just so we can prepare the right materials."

[Note any additional attendees]

"Excellent. We're looking forward to showing you how we can turn those
40 hours a month of manual work into 2 minutes of automated insights.

See you tomorrow at [TIME]!"
```

### Key Points to Cover
- ✅ Confirm time and attendees
- ✅ Set agenda expectations (demo + pricing discussion)
- ✅ Identify any additional stakeholders
- ✅ Reaffirm value proposition (40 hours → 2 minutes)
- ✅ Professional and friendly tone

### Red Flags to Listen For
- ⚠️ Hesitation about meeting ("We might need to reschedule")
- ⚠️ New stakeholders suddenly joining (may indicate buying process)
- ⚠️ Request to "just send pricing via email" (avoidance signal)
- ⚠️ Questions about competitors (comparison shopping)

### If They Want to Reschedule

```
"I understand - things come up. When would work better for you?

Just so I can plan accordingly - is there anything we should adjust
about the meeting? Any specific questions or concerns you'd like us
to address?"

[Listen for real reason - budget, authority, competing priorities]

"No problem. How about [PROPOSE NEW TIME within 3-5 days]?"
```

**Do NOT:**
- Push too hard (creates resistance)
- Ask "why" directly (sounds accusatory)
- Offer to "just send materials" (lose control of process)

## Script #2: Post-Meeting Follow-Up Call

**Timing:** 24-48 hours after negotiation meeting (Oct 18-19, 2025)
**Duration:** 10-15 minutes
**Caller:** Kyrian
**Goal:** Reinforce value, answer questions, advance to next step

### Opening

```
"Hi José Luis, it's Kyrian from AI Whisperers.

I wanted to follow up on our meeting yesterday and see if you had
any additional questions after having time to think about everything
we discussed.

Did you have a chance to review the materials we sent?"

[Wait for response]
```

### If Positive Response

```
"That's great to hear. What stood out most to you from the demo and
our discussion?"

[Listen - this reveals their priorities]

"Exactly - and that automation piece is really where you'll see the
biggest impact. 40 hours of manual work becoming 2 minutes.

Have you had a chance to discuss this internally yet? What's the
next step on your end?"

[Listen for approval process]

"Perfect. To support that conversation, what materials would be most
helpful? We can provide:
- Executive summary (1-page overview)
- Technical specifications
- ROI breakdown
- Implementation timeline
- Security documentation

What would help most?"

[Offer to send tailored materials]

"When would be a good time to reconnect? I want to make sure we can
answer any questions that come up during your internal review."

[Schedule next call]
```

### If Hesitant Response

```
"I understand - this is an important decision. Can I ask what specific
questions or concerns have come up?

[LISTEN CAREFULLY - this is the real objection]

[Use appropriate objection handling script from Objection-Handling-Script.md]

"What would need to happen for you to feel confident moving forward?"

[Uncover real barrier]

"Let me see how we can address that. [PROPOSE SOLUTION]

Would it help if we scheduled a follow-up call to dig into this further?"
```

### If Non-Committal ("We're still thinking")

```
"Absolutely - it's important to think this through.

Can I ask - what specifically are you evaluating right now? Is it:
- The technical fit?
- The pricing and ROI?
- The implementation timeline?
- Something else?

[Identify real concern]

"I appreciate that transparency. Here's what I suggest:

[PROPOSE SPECIFIC NEXT STEP - not just "think about it"]

Options:
1. Pilot program (10 days, 10M PYG, see results immediately)
2. Technical deep-dive call with Jonathan
3. Meeting with your CFO/CTO to address [specific concern]
4. Custom ROI model for your exact scenario

Which would be most helpful?"
```

### Closing

```
"Thanks for your time, José Luis. Just to recap:

- [NEXT STEP 1]
- [NEXT STEP 2]
- We'll reconnect on [DATE/TIME]

I'll send a quick email summarizing this and the materials you requested.

Does that sound good?"

[Confirm and end positively]

"Great. Talk soon!"
```

### Key Outcomes to Achieve
- ✅ Identify any objections or concerns
- ✅ Understand internal approval process
- ✅ Provide requested materials
- ✅ Schedule specific next step (not vague "we'll be in touch")
- ✅ Gauge buying temperature (hot/warm/cold)

## Script #3: Decision Timeline Check-In Call

**Timing:** 5-7 days after meeting (if no decision yet)
**Duration:** 5-10 minutes
**Caller:** Kyrian
**Goal:** Maintain momentum, uncover blockers, support approval process

### Opening

```
"Hi José Luis, it's Kyrian.

I wanted to check in and see how things are progressing on your end.
Have you had a chance to discuss the proposal with [CFO/CTO/Exec Committee]?"

[Wait for status update]
```

### If In-Process

```
"That's great progress. How is the conversation going so far?

[Listen for feedback]

"Is there anything we can provide to help that process move forward?
Additional data, answering specific questions, meeting with other
stakeholders?"

[Offer support]

"What's the timeline looking like for a decision?"

[Get commitment to timeline]

"Perfect. I'll follow up on [DATE]. In the meantime, feel free to reach
out if any questions come up - happy to jump on a call anytime."
```

### If Stalled

```
"I understand these things can take time. Can I ask - what's the
current holdup? Is there something we can help clarify or address?"

[Identify blocker]

Common blockers and responses:

**Blocker: "Waiting for budget approval"**
Response: "I see. Just to clarify - is the budget the only remaining
item, or are there other concerns? Because if budget is the only issue,
we have some flexible options like the pilot program or phased payment."

**Blocker: "Need to get CTO/CFO buy-in"**
Response: "That makes sense - they'll want to understand the technical
details and ROI. Would it be helpful if Jonathan and I joined a call
with them to walk through their specific questions?"

**Blocker: "Comparing other options"**
Response: "Absolutely - you should evaluate your options. Can I ask
what alternatives you're looking at? I want to make sure you're comparing
apples to apples in terms of functionality, timeline, and cost."

**Blocker: "Just busy with other priorities"**
Response: "I completely understand - it's a busy time. Just so I know
where this sits in your priority queue - is this something you still
want to move forward with this quarter, or should we revisit in [NEXT
QUARTER]?"

[Gauge real interest level]
```

### If Decision Made (But Not Communicated)

```
"I appreciate you letting me know. Can I ask - what was the deciding
factor?

[If YES - celebrate and move to contracting]
[If NO - learn and potentially save the deal]

**If NO:**

"I understand. Can I ask what the main concern was? I want to make
sure we didn't miss addressing something important.

[Listen - may still be salvageable]

"Would you be open to one more conversation to address [CONCERN]?
I think we may have a solution you haven't considered."

[Attempt to save - but don't be pushy]

If truly dead:
"I appreciate you taking the time to evaluate our solution. If
circumstances change or you'd like to revisit in the future, we're
here. Best of luck with [ALTERNATIVE CHOSEN]."

[Graceful exit - leave door open]
```

### Closing

```
"Thanks for the update, José Luis. I'll [NEXT STEP] and reconnect
on [DATE].

Feel free to reach out if anything comes up before then."
```

## Script #4: Objection Handling Call

**Timing:** When specific objection arises
**Duration:** 15-20 minutes
**Caller:** Kyrian (+ Jonathan if technical)
**Goal:** Address objection, move deal forward

### Opening

```
"Hi José Luis, thanks for making time.

You mentioned [OBJECTION] in our last conversation. I wanted to dig
into that a bit more so we can address it properly.

Can you tell me more about your concern around [OBJECTION]?"

[LISTEN - let them fully explain]

"I appreciate you sharing that. Let me see if I understand correctly:

[PARAPHRASE THEIR CONCERN]

Is that right?"

[Confirm understanding before responding]
```

### Objection Response Framework

Use the detailed scripts from **Objection-Handling-Script.md** for specific objections:

1. "That's too expensive" → ROI Focus Script
2. "You have no track record" → First Client Advantage Script
3. "We can build in-house" → Total Cost Analysis Script
4. "We need to think about it" → Uncover Real Concern Script
5. "Can we get a discount" → Value First Script
6. "We're talking to competitors" → Differentiation Script

### After Addressing Objection

```
"Does that help address your concern?

[Wait for response]

"What else would you need to see or understand to feel confident
moving forward?"

[Identify remaining barriers]

"Here's what I suggest as next step: [SPECIFIC ACTION]

Does that work for you?"
```

### Closing

```
"Great. So to recap:
- We've addressed [OBJECTION] by [SOLUTION]
- Next step is [ACTION]
- We'll reconnect on [DATE]

Do you feel like we've resolved this, or is there anything else we
should discuss?"

[Ensure objection is fully handled]

"Perfect. Talk to you on [DATE]."
```

## General Phone Call Best Practices

### Tone and Approach
- **Consultative, not salesy:** You're solving a problem, not pushing a product
- **Listen more than talk:** 60/40 rule (them 60%, you 40%)
- **Empathy:** Acknowledge their concerns and constraints
- **Confidence:** You believe in the solution's value
- **Professional warmth:** Friendly but businesslike

### Active Listening Techniques
1. **Paraphrase:** "So what I'm hearing is..."
2. **Clarify:** "Can you tell me more about that?"
3. **Validate:** "That makes sense given..."
4. **Probe:** "What specifically concerns you about...?"

### Question Framework (SPIN)
- **Situation:** "What's your current process for...?"
- **Problem:** "How much time does that take?"
- **Implication:** "What does that cost in terms of...?"
- **Need-Payoff:** "What would it mean if you could reduce that by 99%?"

### Red Flags to Watch For
- ⚠️ Vague answers ("We'll get back to you")
- ⚠️ Avoiding commitment to next step
- ⚠️ New stakeholders appearing (may indicate lack of authority)
- ⚠️ Asking for "best price" immediately (price shopping)
- ⚠️ Comparing feature-for-feature with competitors
- ⚠️ Requesting contract changes that eliminate value

### Green Flags to Leverage
- ✅ Asking detailed implementation questions
- ✅ Introducing you to other stakeholders
- ✅ Discussing timeline and start date
- ✅ Asking about support and training
- ✅ Requesting contract to review
- ✅ Sharing internal budget/approval process

## Emergency Scripts

### If They Say "We Went with Someone Else"

```
"I understand. Can I ask who you decided to go with and what the
deciding factor was? I want to make sure we didn't miss something
important in our proposal.

[Listen]

"I appreciate that feedback. If things don't work out or you'd like
to revisit in the future, we're here. Best of luck with [COMPETITOR]."

[Graceful exit - don't burn bridge]
```

### If They Ask for Extreme Discount (>50%)

```
"I appreciate you being direct about budget constraints.

Unfortunately, we can't go that low and still deliver the quality
and support you need. The investment reflects the R&D, infrastructure,
and ongoing support we provide.

That said, I want to find a way to make this work.

Would a pilot program make sense? [PILOT DETAILS]

Or we could explore a phased approach where you start with [SMALLER
TIER] and expand as you see ROI.

What would work better for your budget right now?"

[Offer alternative structure, not lower price]
```

### If They Go Silent (No Response for 7+ Days)

```
"Hi José Luis, it's Kyrian.

I haven't heard from you in about a week, so I wanted to check in.

I know things get busy - just let me know if you'd like to:
A) Continue the conversation (happy to schedule a call)
B) Revisit this at a later date (no pressure)
C) Close the loop if you've decided not to move forward

Either way, I'd appreciate knowing where things stand so I can plan
accordingly.

Thanks!"

[Give them an easy out - reduces pressure and often gets response]
```
