## SPIN Selling Framework

**S** - Situation Questions (understand current state)
**P** - Problem Questions (uncover pain points)
**I** - Implication Questions (amplify consequences of problems)
**N** - Need-Payoff Questions (envision solution benefits)

---

## SITUATION QUESTIONS (Current State)

### Current Process Understanding

**1. Customer Feedback Volume**
```
"How much customer feedback does Personal receive on a monthly basis?"

Follow-ups:
- What channels? (surveys, support tickets, social media, app reviews)
- What format? (structured surveys, free-text comments, ratings)
- What's the breakdown by source?
```

**2. Current Analysis Method**
```
"Walk me through your current process for analyzing customer feedback.
What does a typical month look like?"

Follow-ups:
- Who's involved in the analysis?
- What tools do you use?
- How long does it take from collecting feedback to generating insights?
- What's the output? (reports, dashboards, presentations)
```

**3. Team Structure**
```
"Who's responsible for customer feedback analysis in your organization?"

Follow-ups:
- How many people?
- Full-time or part-time on this task?
- What are their other responsibilities?
- Who receives the insights? (stakeholders)
```

**4. Decision-Making Process**
```
"How do you currently use feedback insights to make business decisions?"

Follow-ups:
- Who makes decisions based on this data?
- How frequently?
- What types of decisions? (product, service, strategy)
```

**5. Technology Stack**
```
"What tools or systems do you currently use for customer feedback?"

Follow-ups:
- Survey platforms?
- CRM systems?
- Analytics tools?
- Excel/manual processes?
- Any AI or automation?
```

## PROBLEM QUESTIONS (Pain Points)

### Time and Efficiency

**6. Time Investment**
```
"How much time does your team spend on manual feedback analysis each month?"

Follow-ups:
- How many hours per week per person?
- What's the total labor cost?
- What else could they be doing with that time?
- Is this scalable as feedback volume grows?
```

**7. Process Bottlenecks**
```
"What's the most time-consuming part of your current feedback analysis process?"

Follow-ups:
- Reading through comments?
- Categorizing feedback?
- Identifying trends?
- Creating reports?
- Presenting findings?
```

**8. Speed of Insights**
```
"How quickly can you go from collecting feedback to having actionable insights?"

Follow-ups:
- Days? Weeks? Months?
- Is that fast enough for your business needs?
- What happens if you need insights urgently?
```

### Quality and Accuracy

**9. Insight Quality**
```
"How confident are you in the accuracy and completeness of your current
feedback analysis?"

Follow-ups:
- Do you ever miss important trends?
- Can you quantify emotions and sentiment reliably?
- Do you identify all pain points?
- Can you predict churn risk?
```

**10. Human Bias**
```
"How do you ensure objectivity in your current manual analysis process?"

Follow-ups:
- Different analysts interpret differently?
- Unconscious bias in categorization?
- Selective focus on certain types of feedback?
```

### Scalability

**11. Volume Handling**
```
"What happens when feedback volume spikes? Can your current process handle it?"

Follow-ups:
- Do you have to sample rather than analyze all feedback?
- Does analysis take longer?
- Do you need to hire temp staff?
```

**12. Growth Plans**
```
"As Personal grows, how will your feedback analysis process scale?"

Follow-ups:
- Hiring more analysts?
- Investing in technology?
- Current process unsustainable?
```

### Strategic Impact

**13. Customer Churn**
```
"How do you currently identify customers at risk of churning?"

Follow-ups:
- Can you detect early warning signs in feedback?
- Proactive outreach to at-risk customers?
- What's your current churn rate?
- What would 10% churn reduction be worth?
```

**14. NPS Tracking**
```
"Do you track NPS (Net Promoter Score)? How?"

Follow-ups:
- Manual calculation?
- How often updated?
- Broken down by segment/product?
- Trends over time visible?
```

## IMPLICATION QUESTIONS (Amplify Consequences)

### Cost of the Problem

**15. Opportunity Cost**
```
"If your team is spending 40 hours a month on manual analysis, what
strategic work isn't getting done?"

Follow-ups:
- Customer success initiatives?
- Proactive support?
- Product improvement projects?
- Revenue-generating activities?
```

**16. Cost of Missed Insights**
```
"What's the impact when you miss a critical trend or pain point in
customer feedback?"

Follow-ups:
- Product decisions based on incomplete data?
- Customers churn that could have been saved?
- Competitive disadvantage?
- Wasted resources building wrong features?
```

**17. Cost of Slow Response**
```
"What happens when you identify a problem in customer feedback, but
it takes weeks to surface it?"

Follow-ups:
- Problem gets worse?
- More customers affected?
- Reputation damage?
- Revenue loss?
```

**18. Labor Cost Escalation**
```
"As feedback volume grows 20-30% per year, what happens to your
analysis costs?"

Follow-ups:
- Linear growth in labor costs?
- Hiring challenges?
- Training overhead?
- Becomes unsustainable?
```

### Strategic Implications

**19. Competitive Disadvantage**
```
"If your competitors can analyze feedback faster and more accurately,
how does that impact you?"

Follow-ups:
- They respond to customer needs faster?
- They identify opportunities before you?
- Customer experience advantage?
```

**20. Executive Confidence**

```
they in the data?"

Follow-ups:

- Delay decisions?
- Want more frequent updates?
```

## Question Flow Framework

### For Early-Stage Discovery

**Opening (Rapport):**
1. Situation questions (5-7 questions)
2. Problem questions (3-5 questions)

**Middle (Deepen Understanding):**
3. Implication questions (3-4 questions)
4. More problem questions (2-3 questions)

**Closing (Envision Solution):**
5. Need-payoff questions (4-6 questions)
6. Qualifying questions (3-4 questions)
7. Next steps

### For Late-Stage Discovery (Like Personal Paraguay)

Since you've already done discovery, focus on:
- Implication questions (amplify pain)
- Need-payoff questions (envision solution value)
- Qualifying questions (decision process)
- Objection anticipation questions

## Best Practices

### Do:
- ✅ Ask open-ended questions ("Tell me about..." not "Do you...")
- ✅ Listen more than talk (80/20 rule)
- ✅ Ask follow-up questions based on their answers
- ✅ Take detailed notes
- ✅ Confirm understanding by paraphrasing
- ✅ Build on their language and priorities
- ✅ Create conversation, not interrogation

### Don't:
- ❌ Ask leading questions that suggest answers
- ❌ Jump to solution too quickly
- ❌ Interrogate with rapid-fire questions
- ❌ Ignore emotional cues
- ❌ Assume you know their situation
- ❌ Talk more than listen
