## General Thank You Email Best Practices

### When to Send Thank You Emails
- ✅ After every significant interaction (meeting, demo, negotiation)
- ✅ Within 24 hours (ideally within 2-4 hours)
- ✅ After contract signing
- ✅ After receiving feedback, referral, or testimonial
- ✅ Even when you don't win the deal (graceful exit)

### Tone and Structure
- ✅ Genuine gratitude (not performative)
- ✅ Specific references to the interaction
- ✅ Recap value or next steps (not just "thank you")
- ✅ Forward-looking (what's next)
- ✅ Professional but warm

### What to Include
- ✅ Specific thank you for their time/effort
- ✅ What you appreciated or learned
- ✅ Key takeaways or next steps
- ✅ Availability for questions or support
- ✅ Positive, forward-looking close

### What to Avoid
- ❌ Generic template feel (personalize!)
- ❌ Too long (keep it concise)
- ❌ Sales pitch disguised as thank you
- ❌ Asking for too much in a thank you email
- ❌ Being overly effusive (be genuine, not gushy)
