## Meeting Summary Email Best Practices

### Timing
- ✅ Send within 2-24 hours (sooner for critical meetings)
- ✅ Negotiation meetings: within 2 hours
- ✅ Discovery meetings: within 24 hours
- ✅ Regular calls: within 4 hours

### Structure
- ✅ Meeting details (date, time, attendees)
- ✅ Agenda covered (what was discussed)
- ✅ Key points and decisions
- ✅ Action items (theirs and yours)
- ✅ Next steps and timeline
- ✅ Attachments or materials referenced

### Tone
- ✅ Professional and organized
- ✅ Factual and objective
- ✅ Positive and forward-looking
- ✅ Helpful (provides clarity)

### What to Include
- ✅ All significant discussion points
- ✅ Questions asked and answers given
- ✅ Decisions made
- ✅ Commitments (both parties)
- ✅ Materials provided
- ✅ Clear next steps

### What to Avoid
- ❌ Excessive detail (keep it concise)
- ❌ Subjective interpretations
- ❌ Information not discussed in meeting
- ❌ Commitments they didn't actually make
- ❌ Negativity or complaints
