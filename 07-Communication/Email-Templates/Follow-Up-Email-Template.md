## Email Best Practices

### Subject Lines
- ✅ Clear and specific (include client name or context)
- ✅ Action-oriented when appropriate ("Next steps for...")
- ✅ Professional but friendly tone
- ❌ Avoid all caps or excessive punctuation
- ❌ Avoid spammy words ("FREE!", "Limited time!")

### Body Structure
- ✅ Start with gratitude or context
- ✅ Use short paragraphs (3-4 lines max)
- ✅ Use bullet points and formatting for readability
- ✅ Clear call-to-action
- ✅ End with availability and support offer

### Tone
- ✅ Professional but warm
- ✅ Helpful and consultative (not salesy)
- ✅ Confident but not pushy
- ✅ Empathetic to their process and constraints

### Timing
- ✅ Immediate follow-up: Within 2 hours of meeting
- ✅ Check-in: 5-7 days after no response
- ✅ Secondary check-in: 10-14 days after first check-in
- ✅ Graceful exit: 14-21 days of silence

### Attachments
- ✅ Reference all attachments in email body
- ✅ Use clear file names (not "Document1.pdf")
- ✅ PDF for polished documents, Excel for interactive models
- ✅ Keep total size under 10MB
