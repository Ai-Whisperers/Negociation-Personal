## Proposal Email Best Practices

### Structure
- ✅ Executive summary (problem, solution, ROI)
- ✅ Package recommendation with clear rationale
- ✅ Alternative options (give choice)
- ✅ Detailed investment breakdown
- ✅ ROI analysis with multiple metrics
- ✅ Implementation timeline (clear and fast)
- ✅ Deliverables (what they get)
- ✅ Terms and conditions (transparent)
- ✅ Risk mitigation (address concerns)
- ✅ Clear next steps
- ✅ Attached materials (support decision)

### Tone
- ✅ Professional and detailed
- ✅ Consultative (not salesy)
- ✅ Data-driven (numbers and facts)
- ✅ Confident (believe in value)
- ✅ Helpful (make decision easy)

### What to Include
- ✅ All pricing options clearly explained
- ✅ ROI calculated multiple ways
- ✅ Implementation timeline (speed matters)
- ✅ Comparison to alternatives
- ✅ Risk mitigation strategies
- ✅ Attachments that support each stakeholder
- ✅ Clear call-to-action

### What to Avoid
- ❌ Overwhelming detail (balance thoroughness with readability)
- ❌ Pushy sales language
- ❌ Hidden costs or vague pricing
- ❌ Unrealistic promises
- ❌ Ignoring alternatives or competition
