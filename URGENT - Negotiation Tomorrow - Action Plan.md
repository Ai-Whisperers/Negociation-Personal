### B. Objection Responses (30 min)

**Prepare responses for these:**

**1. "That's expensive"**
```
"I understand it's an investment. Let me show you the ROI:
You're currently spending [X hours/week] on [manual process].
That's [Y] PYG annually just in time costs.

This solution pays for itself in [Z] months and saves you
[amount] every year after that.

Is a [Z]-month payback acceptable for your business?"
```

**2. "We can build this in-house"**
```
"Absolutely, many companies can. The question is time-to-value
and opportunity cost.

Most in-house projects take 6-12 months and cost 2-3x the estimate.
We're ready now - you can be live in 2 months.

What's the cost of waiting 6-12 months?"
```

**3. "We need to think about it"**
```
"Of course, this is an important decision.

To help your thinking, what specific concerns or questions
can I address today?

I want to make sure you have everything you need to decide."
```

**4. "Can you add [feature X]?"**
```
"That's a great idea for a future enhancement.

To keep timeline and budget on track, I recommend launching
with [core features] first, then adding [feature X] as Phase 2.

We can discuss that pricing separately. Sound good?"
```

**5. "We have other vendors quoting lower"**
```
"I appreciate you shopping around - that's smart.

Let's compare apples to apples: what's included in their quote?
- Implementation timeline?
- Support level?
- Customization?
- Ongoing updates?

Often lower prices mean less support or longer timelines.
I'm confident our value justifies the investment."
```

# 📞 IF THINGS GO WRONG

**If they say "That's way too expensive":**
→ "I understand. Let me ask: what budget range were you expecting?"
→ Then decide if you can work within it (≥15 units)

**If they want to "think about it":**
→ "Of course. What specific concerns can I address today?"
→ "When would be a good time to reconnect?"

**If they try to push you below 15 units:**
→ "I appreciate your position. To deliver the value we discussed,
   my minimum engagement is 15 units. Below that, I can't sustain
   the quality and support you need."
→ Then STOP TALKING. Let them respond.

**If they want unlimited scope:**
→ "Let's be clear on what's included in [package] vs. what would
   be additional. Here's what [package] covers: [list].
   Additional features can be quoted separately."

**If you need to pause:**
→ "Can we take a 5-minute break? I want to make sure I'm giving
   you the best possible solution."
→ Step away, regroup, come back with clarity

**If you need to walk away:**
→ "I appreciate your time today. Based on [constraints], this might
   not be the right fit right now. I'd love to stay in touch if
   circumstances change."
→ Leave professionally, door open
