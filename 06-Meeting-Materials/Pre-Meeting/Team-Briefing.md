## Negotiation Strategy

### Core Approach: Reverse-Anchor

**Primary Tactic:**
1. **Ask them first:** "What budget range were you considering?"
2. **If they give a number:** Respond based on playbook matrix
3. **If they dodge:** Present tier structure (S/M/L), typical range 40-80M

**Why This Works:**
- They might anchor HIGHER than our target
- We're not constrained by low expectation
- Shows confidence
- We learn their budget reality

### Response Matrix

| Their Anchor | Our Response |
|--------------|--------------|
| ≥100M PYG | Accept + add value ✅ |
| 80-100M | Accept (target range) ✅ |
| 60-80M | Work within range ✅ |
| 40-60M | Counteroffer or Medium tier ⚠️ |
| 15-40M | Pilot model or walk ⚠️ |
| <15M | Hard pass ❌ |

### Tier Structure

**Small (40M PYG):** Testing the waters
**Medium (60M PYG):** Regular usage
**Large (80M PYG):** Full deployment
**Pilot (20-30M PYG):** 10-day proof of value

**Flexibility:** Payment terms, features, support duration
**Firmness:** Price floor (40M), IP protection, scope limits
