## Delivery Tips

### Voice & Pacing
- **Vary your pace** - Slow for key numbers, faster for context
- **Pause** - After big statements, let them sink in
- **Volume** - Emphasize key points with slight volume increase
- **Energy** - Match their energy, don't overwhelm

### Body Language
- **Eye contact** - José Luis primarily, acknowledge others
- **Open posture** - No crossed arms
- **Hand gestures** - Natural, not excessive
- **Movement** - Minimal, stay grounded

### Engagement
- **Watch for signals** - Nodding, leaning in, note-taking
- **Ask questions** - "Does this resonate?" "What questions?"
- **Let them interrupt** - Don't steamroll
- **Active listening** - When they talk, really listen

### Energy Management
- **Start strong** - First 2 minutes set tone
- **Build to demo** - Demo is the excitement peak
- **Sustain through ROI** - This is the logical peak
- **Close with vision** - End on inspiration
