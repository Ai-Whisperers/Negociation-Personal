## Potential Blockers & Mitigation

### Blocker 1: Budget Not Available
**Risk:** 3/5 (Medium)

**Mitigation:**
- Offer payment terms (50%/30%/20% over 60 days)
- Start with pilot (lower entry cost)
- Show ROI calculator (pays for itself in 2.4 months)
- Position as investment, not cost

### Blocker 2: CTO Wants to Build In-House
**Risk:** 2/5 (Low) - but could derail deal

**Mitigation:**
- Show timeline: 6-12 months in-house vs. 2 weeks with us
- Show cost: $135k in-house vs. $5,714 with us
- Show opportunity cost: Their devs can build revenue features instead
- Offer source code (they get it anyway, why wait 12 months?)

### Blocker 3: Slow Approval Process
**Risk:** 4/5 (High) - bureaucracy delays

**Mitigation:**
- Pilot model (lower risk = faster approval)
- Executive summary (speeds decision-making)
- Clear ROI and payback period (easy yes)
- Stay in touch with José Luis during approval

### Blocker 4: Competition (Other Vendors)
**Risk:** 3/5 (Medium)

**Mitigation:**
- Price advantage (90-98% cheaper)
- Speed advantage (ready now vs. 3-6 months implementation)
- Source code included (vs. vendor lock-in)
- Comparison matrix (show our advantages)

### Blocker 5: José Luis Leaves Company
**Risk:** 2/5 (Low) - but catastrophic if happens

**Mitigation:**
